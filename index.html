09Five moves, in order

This is paperwork, not a marketing program

  1. Get on the list AI reads most. Join the International OCD Foundation directory. Worth one phone call to confirm you are not already listed.
  2. Get on the list for your strongest niche. The TLC Foundation directory for skin picking and hair pulling. Check first whether you are already on it; their therapist finder only shows results in a browser, so we could not check for you.
  3. Correct the old records. Fix the Psychology Today city, merge the duplicate Zocdoc pages, retire the dead booking page and the delisted profile, put your practice name on BetterHelp or close it.
  4. Claim your Google listing. The address can stay hidden. It gives patients and machines one card you control.
  5. Add the one missing state page: Florida. Your website already has pages for Texas, Washington, and New Hampshire. Florida, where you can also see patients, has no page yet.

You have already done the hard part on your own site: state pages, and real writing on taboo thoughts, emetophobia, and skin picking. That work is why Gemini describes you so accurately when it finds you. What is missing is the layer above it: the lists machines trust.

None of this changes what patients see or how you practice. It makes sure every list the machines read has you on file, correctly.
