Geographic markets and demographics

Four state markets, gated by licensure

Because sessions are virtual and licensure is state-gated, this practice competes in four state-level markets. Patients rarely type a state into a search; geography is applied for them by machines, through "near me" localization, directory state filters, and AI assistants that ask where the patient lives before recommending anyone. Method

What we asked the AI assistants, and why

The question set mirrors the patient funnel described above. Three questions measure the education stage, where no geography exists yet. Twelve measure the recommendation moment, with the state injected the way an AI conversation injects it: six for Texas, three for Washington, one each for New Hampshire and Florida, and one that asks how to choose between platforms and a private specialist. Every question is traceable to documented patient language: Google Autocomplete completions harvested on November 26 (examples below), the IOCDF's published guidance on finding an ERP therapist, and the decision patterns visible in patient communities.

The positioning does not need to change. It needs to be present in the sources the engines read.
